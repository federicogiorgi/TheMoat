# The Moat - RimWorld Mod
*by Keldarton*

**The Moat** is a custom C# Harmony mod for RimWorld that forces your **starting base map** (and *only* that base) to generate with 50% impassable mountain covering the western half, leaving the eastern half as completely flat, lake-free open plains.

---

## Highlights

- **Left 50% (West Half):**
  - **100% Impassable Mountain:** Filled from top to bottom with solid natural rock walls.
  - **Overhead Mountain Roof:** Every tile is covered by `RoofRockThick`.
  - **No Caves & No Hollow Spots:** Strictly zero caves and zero hidden pockets.
  - **No Special Features inside the Mountain:** Ancient dangers, shrines, monoliths, steam geysers, and ruins are strictly barred from spawning inside or on the mountain.
- **Right 50% (East Half):**
  - **No Mountains or Hills:** Elevation is kept completely flat.
  - **No Lakes:** Any ponds, lakes, rivers, or marshes are automatically converted into dry land matching local biome fertility.
  - **All Normal Features Spawn:** Dirt/soil varieties, wild trees, grass, animals, steam geysers, ancient dangers, ruins, roads, and the Anomaly Void Monolith all generate cleanly across the plains.
  - **Safe Player Landing:** Colonists and drop pods always arrive safely on the open plains.
- **Single-Base Scope:**
  - Affects **only** your initial starting colony map.
  - Any future colonies, quest locations, encounter maps, ancient complexes, or campsites generate completely standard vanilla terrain!
- **Mod Settings:**
  - Adjustable mountain width slider (default: 50%).
  - Toggle to allow or disallow mineable ore veins inside the mountain rock.
  - Toggle to remove water/lakes on the plains.
  - Toggle to flatten hills on the plains.

---

## Installation & How to Play

1. The mod is already installed in your RimWorld directory:
   `E:\SteamGames\Steam\steamapps\common\RimWorld\Mods\TheMoat`
2. Start **RimWorld**.
3. In the main menu, click **Mods**.
4. Enable **Harmony** (make sure it is loaded near the top), then check **The Moat**.
5. Click **Close** to reload mods.
6. Start a **New Game** on any world tile.
7. Your starting colony will spawn facing the massive mountain barrier on the left!
